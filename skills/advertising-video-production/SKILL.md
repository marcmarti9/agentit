# Advertising Video Production

Own the creative production of a video ad. Turn product truth and a campaign
objective into a timed, editable deliverable. `marketing-and-growth` owns
positioning, channel strategy and learning; renderer specialists own their
implementation contracts. Neither becomes a mandatory companion.

## Establish the usable brief

Inspect the existing brief, product, brand and campaign evidence first. Record
audience situation, offer, intended action, distribution surface, output
language, duration, aspect ratios, available assets and audio expectations.
Use supplied choices; make reversible draft assumptions explicit. Ask only for
missing facts that materially block production, not a fixed questionnaire.

Maintain a compact ledger for material that will appear:

| Item | Source and date | Status | Production use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product action / price / claim | File, product observation or approved brief | Observed, supplied, inferred, unresolved | Exact approved wording or blocked proof |
| Screenshot / logo / footage / font / audio / performer | Asset ID and provenance | Supplied, licensed, generated, permission unresolved | Allowed context, restrictions or draft placeholder |

Keep factual verification separate from permission to reuse. A public listing
is a research source, not a commercial asset license. Existing client-owned or
approved assets may be used within the task's authority without asking again.
Do not invent reviews, results, discounts, scarcity, metrics or personal
experience. A generated actor or product image does not establish product proof.
If proof is absent, show a truthful mechanism/demo or choose a different angle.

## Choose an idea that belongs to this product

Start with a source-backed audience tension, useful demonstration or approved
offer. Explore materially different concepts when the brief needs exploration;
an exact supplied concept needs implementation, not another idea ceremony.

For each candidate name the visual premise, promise, evidence, payoff and next
action. Compare product fit, clarity, distinctiveness, feasibility and claim
support. A striking frame should advance the message; do not prescribe constant
shakes, grain, transitions, exaggerated before/after or a universal bold style.

Write the opening as three coordinated tracks: **visual action, spoken line
(if any), on-screen text**. Let each add information. Accessibility captions
must faithfully reproduce speech even when a separate headline adds context;
do not remove necessary captions to satisfy an anti-duplication aesthetic rule.
The following beat must pay off the opening's premise. Choose timing from the
message, audience, channel and total duration rather than a fixed 15-second arc.

Read [creative-variants.md](references/creative-variants.md) when comparing
angles, generating a batch or diagnosing measured creative performance.

## Make the production plan concrete

Create a timed storyboard before complex execution. For a simple approved
edit, the existing timeline/edit decision list can serve this purpose.

| Beat / time | Viewer learns | Visual and asset IDs | Motion / cut | Speech / text | Audio / hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Why this is relevant | Specific action or evidence | Intentional reveal | Supported premise | Sound-off meaning intact |
| Development | How it works / why believe | Actual demo, detail or approved illustration | Preserve causal/spatial relationship | Evidence and qualifications | Enough reading time |
| Decision | What to do next | Offer and destination | Stable readable ending | CTA consistent with landing page | Ending survives platform trim |

These are jobs, not mandatory scenes. One shot can serve multiple jobs. Mark
mocks/illustrations and keep them visually distinguishable from actual product
behavior. Preserve qualifiers next to relevant claims and allow enough time to
read them. Do not animate counters through unsupported values or omit a unit.

Specify dimensions, frame rate, time units, duration, safe areas, brand tokens,
asset IDs, shot crop, caption timing, audio sources and editable inputs. Decide
whether each deliverable needs a separate layout: vertical, square and landscape
usually need reframing/reflow, not a stretched master. Check current platform
requirements when the destination matters; do not fossilize them into this skill.

## Produce through the selected route

Use the user's choice or suitable existing project stack. If the route itself
is unresolved, investigate the capabilities needed; do not install a renderer
or invoke a paid media service merely because a skill mentions it.

- Motion/type/UI demonstration: authored composition or approved screen footage.
- Creator/product shoot: an edit decision list using supplied footage.
- Generated or hybrid footage: read [generated-and-creator-media.md](references/generated-and-creator-media.md)
  only when synthetic/creator media is actually part of the deliverable.
- Speech, captions or a soundtrack: read [audio-and-captions.md](references/audio-and-captions.md)
  only when these tracks are in scope.

Keep approved copy and assets editable; avoid code per variant or untraceable
hand edits. Preview the riskiest opening, proof beat, transition and ending
before spending on full-resolution batches. Ordinary authorized local rendering
can proceed; a paid call, publication or new external permission still needs its
own authority. Prior authorization persists across revisions.

## Verify and hand off

Inspect representative playback as well as stills. Check actual product truth,
reading time, caption/speech agreement, sound-off comprehension, crop and safe
areas, cut continuity, audio peaks/sync and final CTA/destination agreement.
After encoding, probe the actual output's dimensions, duration, frame rate and
streams, decode representative frames from that file and check its beginning
and end. A storyboard or editor preview cannot establish final-file correctness.

Deliver the editable project/timeline, final files, brief/claim/asset ledger,
variant IDs, exact observed QA coverage and remaining gaps. Explain what the
client can safely change and how to regenerate; no undocumented dependency on
the implementer for routine copy, asset or layout variants.

Production approval and channel performance are different outcomes. Report
rendered evidence precisely; call an ad a winner only with relevant observed
campaign data, population, measurement window and uncertainty.
