# Motion, video and advertising skill expansion — October 4, 2026

## Result and boundary

The extension adds three selectively loaded capabilities to the preceding
93-skill/18-profile design expansion: **96 skills, 19 profiles**, with exactly
three global core bodies. The adaptation manifest now has 23 fixed source
records. Six licensed sources inform this extension: five new records and the
existing Corey Haines record extended at its already fixed revision.

- `motion-graphics-production`: authored rendered-video craft, storyboard/key
  poses, type/logo/product scenes, variants and final-output review.
- `remotion-video-engineering`: actual selected Remotion project, current local
  API contracts, deterministic scene clocks, assets/fonts/props and encoding.
- `advertising-video-production`: audience/offer-to-video creative production,
  factual proof, hooks, timed scenes, captions/audio, variants and handoff.
- `creative-tool-scout` gains a conditional video-route comparison for
  Hyperframes, existing AE, offline 3D, NLE/FFmpeg, Rive/Lottie and hybrid footage.

An ad selects its ad-production owner; a non-ad motion piece selects its motion
production owner. A selected Remotion project may justify the technical adapter.
No production task automatically loads marketing strategy, every design skill
or another renderer. Existing Emil/GSAP/Three.js web creation, web narrative
and `motion-design-review` keep their distinct scopes and canonical packages.

This is wider source coverage and implemented guidance, not evidence of
universal superiority or higher ad conversion. No paid generation, provider
connection, upstream installer/script, ad-account mutation or publication ran.

## Production methods and optional context

Exact branded typography/UI/product behavior favors a controlled composition
or real screen capture. Supplied creator/product footage favors an edit list.
Generated imagery/footage can supply illustrative atmosphere; precise claims,
logos and UI remain controlled overlays when synthesis cannot preserve them.
No synthetic presenter is treated as a real customer testimonial.

Branch references are read only when needed:

- Motion beat-sheet/time conversion, ratio reflow and render evidence.
- Remotion version, timeline boundaries, assets/layouts and final decode checks.
- Ad concept/variant learning, generated/creator/hybrid media, audio/captions.
- Tool-scout video alternatives and existing AE operating constraints.

The `video` profile and pack are optional metadata surfaces. `design`, `growth`
and `all` expose relevant additions; core/default/global membership is unchanged.
Client delivery keeps copy, assets and supported variants editable, with a
regeneration handoff instead of routine dependence on the implementer.
