# Cognitive-comprehension review

Use only for the trigger named in the parent skill. This branch reviews whether
the interface adds avoidable cognitive work; it does not diagnose a person or
declare WCAG conformance.

For each screen or decision point, walk it as a first-time user who may be
tired, interrupted, or under stress. Record:

| Screen/state | Required goal | Decisions | Recall burden | New concepts | Steps remaining | Reading/visual burden | Evidence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|

Classify the burden before recommending change:

- **Intrinsic:** necessary complexity of the task or regulated domain. Explain
  it, segment it, and keep essential context visible.
- **Extraneous:** complexity caused by inconsistent labels, hidden state,
  duplicate choices, unclear next actions, irrelevant detail, or recall the
  interface could avoid. This is the primary repair target.
- **Germane:** effort spent understanding or completing the user's real goal.
  Preserve it while removing extraneous burden.

Recommend only a traceable reduction: progressive disclosure, grouping,
recognition over recall, stable vocabulary, persistent selected context,
useful defaults, predictable next steps, or specific error recovery. Record
the product consequence and who must validate a trade-off. Do not claim that a
count alone proves a barrier, simplify away necessary review, or substitute an
invented persona for user research.
