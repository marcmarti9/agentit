# Creative variants and learning

Read for multiple ad concepts, controlled batches or performance diagnosis.
The main skill owns production; the existing marketing owner owns investment
and measurement decisions. This reference does not authorize ad-account edits.

Build a small matrix from the evidence actually available: audience situation,
motivation/source, creative form, opening action, spoken line, headline,
supporting beat and proof. Useful forms include a product demo, creator
demonstration, screen recording, kinetic type, comparison or offer explanation.
Choose forms for the mechanism; a talking head and a motion collage are not
universal defaults. Caption faithful speech separately from persuasive headlines.

Change a meaningful axis: audience tension, demonstration, evidence, offer
framing or opening action. Recoloring one template or paraphrasing one sentence
does not test different concepts. For an opening test, preserve the premise
through the next beat; blindly attaching every hook to an unchanged body can
invalidate the comparison. Record necessary dependent changes as part of the
treatment, rather than pretending that only one element changed.

Keep a stable control, variant ID, hypothesis and asset/copy version. Scale
production fidelity to the evidence and decision: draft stills/animatics for
unresolved ideas, fuller execution for approved or promising concepts. Do not
invent a fixed minimum number of concepts, thresholds or a spend ladder.

Inspect the complete funnel with the channel's actual event definitions:

| Evidence | Possible investigation, not a diagnosis by itself |
|---|---|
| Poor opening retention | Opening relevance, visibility and audience match |
| Good opening, weak later retention | Premise payoff, pacing, clarity and length |
| Viewing without qualified action | Offer/proof/CTA, audience intent or placement |
| Clicks without conversion | Ad/landing consistency, friction, attribution or offer |

Use comparable audiences, placements, attribution windows and sample sizes.
Platform event definitions differ; verify them before comparing rates. A
high click rate can coexist with low-quality traffic. A single correlation
does not prove which frame caused the change. Store results and the next
production decision, including when the right action is to stop.
