# Marketing evidence and learning playbook

Read this for a material positioning, campaign, launch, CRO or customer-research decision when the main marketing reference needs a compact method for turning customer evidence into a measurable test. Do not load it for a one-off rewrite with sufficient approved context.

## Start with the decision, not the channel

Set the business decision the work must inform: choose an ICP, revise a message, fund a channel, improve a conversion step, or decide whether a launch is ready. Capture the offer, intended customer, current alternative, available proof, operating constraint, target behavior and review window. If the proposed claim cannot be supported by product truth or customer evidence, remove or qualify it before creative production.

## Build a traceable customer-evidence set

Use three evidence modes deliberately:

1. **Existing internal material:** interviews, sales calls, support, churn notes, usage and cohort data.
2. **Public discovery:** reviews, communities, competitor discussions and search results. Social posts are leads and quoted customer language, not representative market proof.
3. **Primary research:** interviews, surveys, prototype tests or offer tests when existing evidence cannot settle the decision.

For each observation retain the source URL or internal identifier, date, segment cues, verbatim language where lawful, context, theme and bias/limitation. Cluster themes only after collecting individual observations. Frequency without severity can hide an urgent problem; intensity without a segment can amplify an outlier.

## Turn a theme into an accountable experiment

```text
evidence-backed customer problem:
segment and current alternative:
message / offer / journey hypothesis:
mechanism and proof shown:
funnel stage being changed:
primary metric and guardrail metric:
baseline, exposure cap and decision threshold:
owner, review date and next action for pass / fail / inconclusive:
```

Prefer the smallest honest test that can change the decision. A campaign that measures only reach cannot establish product demand; a landing-page conversion change cannot prove long-term retention. Keep the causal claim proportionate to the metric actually observed.

## Feed learning into the operating system

Record outcome separately from interpretation: result, sample/period, operational changes, confidence, alternative explanations and the next decision. Reuse confirmed customer language in copy only with its meaning intact. Refresh the evidence when the audience, offer, market or channel mechanism changes.
