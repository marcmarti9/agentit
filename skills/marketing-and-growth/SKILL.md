# Marketing and Growth Engineering

## Core Principles

1. **Clarity Over Buzzwords**: Communicate clear value propositions instantly. Avoid jargon and generic claims.
2. **Technical SEO Foundation**: Ensure fast page load times, semantic HTML5 tags, structured JSON-LD data, canonical URLs, and dynamic OpenGraph/Twitter card metadata.
3. **Data-Driven Funnels**: Instrument event tracking at key conversion touchpoints before launching campaigns.

## Key Areas

### 1. Technical SEO & Social Cards
- **OpenGraph & Twitter Meta Tags**: Every page must define `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:url`, `twitter:card`, and `twitter:image`.
- **Structured Data**: Include Schema.org JSON-LD scripts for Organization, WebSite, Product, or Article schemas.
- **Sitemap & Robots**: Maintain `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt` with clear crawl rules.

### 2. Copywriting & Value Positioning
- **Hero Headline**: State what the product does and for whom in 1 sentence.
- **Call-to-Action (CTA)**: Use action-oriented, low-friction button text ("Start Free Trial", "View on GitHub", "Explore Interactive Demo").
- **Social Proof**: Highlight real metrics, user testimonials, badges, or GitHub star counts.

### 3. CRO & Funnel Optimization
- **Friction Reduction**: Minimize required form fields.
- **Analytics Events**: Track `page_view`, `cta_click`, `signup_start`, `signup_complete`, and `feature_used`.
