# Generated, creator and hybrid media

Read when the selected ad needs filmed, synthetic or mixed footage. Select a
production route by required truth and controllability, not the newest model.

| Route | Useful fit | Required checks |
|---|---|---|
| Real creator / founder / product footage | Actual demonstration, lived experience, precise product appearance | Source consent, approved statements, usage scope, edit continuity |
| Synthetic presenter | Clearly framed dramatization or narration | Authorized identity/voice, disclosure requirements, approved script; no fake testimonial |
| Image-to-video | Art-directed atmosphere or a short shot from an approved keyframe | Keyframe rights, identity/product drift, temporal stability, crop and trim handles |
| Text-to-video | Illustrative setting where exact UI/text/product geometry is unnecessary | Current provider capabilities, iteration budget, artifacts and factual ambiguity |
| Hybrid | Footage plus exact brand/type/UI overlays | Separate controllable factual layer, color/light/framing continuity |
| Static or carousel derivative | Evidence needs reading time or low-cost concept testing | Genuine medium adaptation; do not count as a verified video export |

Plan shot purpose, duration/handles, framing, reference assets, continuity
anchors and what must remain exact. Test the highest-risk shot before a batch.
Check product geometry, brand marks, hands, identities, lighting, camera
direction and transition continuity across frames. Put precise claims, text,
logos and UI in a controlled overlay when the generator cannot reproduce them.
An illustrative device screen must not impersonate unavailable functionality.

Inspect current tools and account authority before generation. Separate cost
estimate, approved spend/retry limit, source-upload scope and asset approval.
Avoid sending private customer material to a service without authorization.
Do not auto-connect an MCP, choose a billed account or silently regenerate an
entire batch. Do not ask again when a suitable budget/action is already approved.

Never script a fictional speaker's personal use/result as a real customer's
experience. Authentic testimony needs its actual source and permission;
dramatized narration must not imply that it is authentic testimony. Current
channel/jurisdiction/provider disclosure and rights rules require live checks
when materially relevant. No skill or generated label is a legal clearance.

Reference mechanisms such as contrast, reveal or demo can inspire new work;
do not reproduce protected footage, scripts, distinctive sequences, likeness,
voice or music. Export the editable assembly, shot/asset provenance, actual
review coverage and unresolved synthetic-media limitations.
