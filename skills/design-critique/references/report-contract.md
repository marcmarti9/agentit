# Broad critique report contract

Read only for an explicitly requested multi-surface or multi-lens critique.
This procedure shapes the report; it does not activate every possible lens.

1. Declare target revision, reviewed routes/states/viewports/themes and the brief.
   State unavailable inputs and distinguish provided captures from fresh captures.
2. Keep an **action list** with stable IDs, audience impact, priority, location,
   concrete repair, proposed owner/role, effort basis or unknown, and retest.
3. Keep a separate **coverage ledger**: what each selected lens inspected, how,
   evidence type, tool/version where applicable, and unassessed states. Cross-link
   findings to evidence; do not repeat them as new defects in every section.
4. Prioritize impact first, then confidence and dependencies. A quick visual fix
   must not outrank loss of data or inability to complete a task just because it
   takes less time. Do not convert ordinal labels into pretend precise scores.
5. Retain binding brand decisions and supported strengths. Mark opinion as
   opinion and suggest a comparison when intent cannot be resolved from evidence.
6. Include exact original text for copy rewrites. Neither an attractive rewrite
   nor a generated mockup establishes a factual business claim.
7. Before delivery, check every finding for location, impact, evidence, repair and
   retest; merge duplicate root causes. Missing evidence becomes a pending check,
   never a manufactured measurement or compliance failure.

Example coverage:

| Scope | Evidence | What it supports | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing, light theme, supplied 390px capture | Rendered observation | Visible hierarchy and wrapping | Capture freshness unknown |
| Submit handler, current source revision | Source-confirmed fact | Missing pending guard in this handler | Runtime behavior untested |
| Keyboard walkthrough, listed browser/version | Manual execution | Focus behavior in tested flow | Other flows and AT untested |

Report modifications from Crit: removed fixed six-lens scope, mandatory tools,
legal assertions, universal scores/effort hours and automatic report publication.
